How it plays

A small light circles a moss island. Tap anywhere and it leaps straight ahead toward the next ring. Time it well, fly through the golden seeds and make the bud bloom. Every garden brings its own ideas: thorns, drifting and fragile islands, currents, comets, gates, frost, portals, switches, night, gravity wells and a rising blight.

When you clear danger by a fraction of a second, time slows for a moment and the margin floats up, like 0.06 s. A miss costs a second, not a menu.
