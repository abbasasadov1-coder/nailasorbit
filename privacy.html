Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Naila’s Orbit is a game for iPhone made by Abbas Asadov. This policy covers the version of Naila’s Orbit on the App Store.

In short: Naila’s Orbit does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What the game stores, and where

  • Your progress (cleared levels, seeds, best times, medals) and your settings (language, sound, vibration, reduce motion, lantern color) are stored only on your iPhone, in the game’s own storage, and in your own iCloud or computer backups if you use them. They are never sent to me or to anyone else.
  • Deleting the game deletes this data. You can also erase your progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress.

What the game does not do

  • No account and no sign-in of its own (Game Center is optional, see below).
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code (SDKs).
  • No server of mine: the game works fully offline. Its only network use is Apple’s Game Center, when you are signed in to it.
  • No permissions: no camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game reports your achievement progress and your Thorn trial clear times to Apple’s Game Center. Apple keeps them and shows them to you and, on the leaderboards, to other players under your Game Center nickname. This is Apple’s service, covered by Apple’s privacy policy. The game sends none of it to me. In App Store Connect I can see a leaderboard’s top entries (nickname and time) and remove cheated scores. To play without Game Center, decline the sign-in or sign out in Settings → Game Center.

Information Apple shares with developers

Apple may share anonymous, aggregated statistics with me, such as downloads, crash reports and usage, only from people who chose to share them with app developers. This is governed by Apple’s privacy policy, and I cannot identify individual players from it.

Test versions (TestFlight)

Test builds distributed through TestFlight also keep a play log on the device: levels started, cleared and lost, attempts, hits and falls, when the hint hand appeared, time played, the days the game was opened, the phone model and iOS version. The log holds no name, email or contacts, is excluded from backups and is never sent automatically. A tester can share it by hand from Settings → Test log. Logs that testers send me are used only to improve the levels and are deleted when that work is done. App Store versions keep no play log. Apple’s TestFlight terms also apply.

Children

Naila’s Orbit is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting data

Because the game collects no personal data, I hold nothing to keep or delete. To remove all game data, delete the game from your iPhone. If you sent me a TestFlight play log, write to me and I will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: write to me on GitHub
