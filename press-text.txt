Naila's Orbit - press text

One line: A one-tap orbit game: leap from ring to ring and slip past danger by a hair, across 200 handcrafted levels in 10 moss gardens.

Short: Naila's Orbit is a quiet one-tap game full of very close calls. A small light circles a moss island; tap and it leaps to the next ring. Clear danger by a fraction of a second and time slows while the margin floats up: 0.06 s. 200 handcrafted levels across 10 moss gardens, free, with no ads.

Facts: iPhone (iOS 17+). Release 14 January 2027, worldwide; pre-orders from early December 2026. Free: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no data collected. Seven languages. Game Center: 37 achievements, a leaderboard for each of the 30 Thorn trials. Developer: Abbas Asadov, solo, Baku, Azerbaijan.
