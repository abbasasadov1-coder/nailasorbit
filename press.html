Short description

Naila’s Orbit is a quiet one-tap game full of very close calls. A small light circles a moss island; tap and it leaps to the next ring. Clear danger by a fraction of a second and time slows while the margin floats up: 0.06 s. 200 handcrafted levels across 10 moss gardens, free, with no ads.

Long description

Tap once to leap from orbit to orbit and slip past danger by a hair. A small light circles a moss island. Tap anywhere and it leaps straight ahead toward the next ring. Time it well, fly through the golden seeds and make the bud bloom.

Every garden brings its own ideas: thorns, drifting and fragile islands, currents, comets, gates, frost, portals, switches, night, gravity wells and a rising blight. Later levels are built around a decision: which route, which stone, when to let go.

When you clear danger by a fraction of a second, time slows for a moment and the margin floats up, like 0.06 s. A miss costs a second, not a menu: you are back on the ring almost at once. Gold Bloom asks for a flawless run with every seed under par time, Thorn trials test steady hands against Game Center leaderboards, and a daily garden remixes a level you have already bloomed.

All 200 levels are free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account, and the game plays offline.

Features

  • 200 handcrafted levels in 10 moss gardens, each with its own rules and music
  • Near misses shown to the hundredth of a second, in slow motion
  • Three seeds of light in every level: on the way, off the path and near the thorns
  • Gold Bloom for a flawless run; 30 Thorn trials with Game Center leaderboards
  • A daily garden: a mirrored remix of a level you have bloomed
  • Help when you need it, never for sale: a ghost hand shows the timing, and a level can be skipped after several tries
  • Reduce motion, one-finger play and VoiceOver labels in seven languages

About the developer

Abbas Asadov makes Naila’s Orbit alone in Baku, Azerbaijan: design, code, levels and music. Every one of the 200 levels was designed and tuned by hand, and each garden has its own original soundtrack.

FAQ

Did you use AI?

Every level is designed by hand, the visuals are drawn by code I wrote with no AI image generator, and the music is my own. I used AI coding assistants while programming.

Can I stream or make videos?

Yes. You may stream Naila’s Orbit and monetize your videos. A link to the game is appreciated.

How do I get a build?

The public TestFlight beta is open: testflight.apple.com/join/GVx8bNuR. Please keep reviews for after 14 January 2027.
