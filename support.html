How to play

You circle a ring. Tap anywhere on the screen to leap straight ahead to the next ring, and reach the bud to make it bloom. Collect the seeds of light: they open the next garden. If a level is hard, a ghost hand can show you the timing, and after several tries you can skip it (garden finals excepted).

Language

Settings (gear icon) → Language. The game plays in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese.

Oyunu Azərbaycan dilində oynamaq üçün: Settings → Language → Azərbaycan dili.

Progress

Your progress is stored on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore an iCloud or computer backup; there is no account and no cloud sync. Settings → Reset progress erases it (settings are kept).

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center) to earn achievements and to see your Thorn trial times on the leaderboards. A trial’s result screen has a Leaderboard button, and the game’s Settings show your achievements. Game Center is optional: the whole game plays without it.

Accessibility

  • Reduce motion (Settings): turns off slow motion, screen shake and zoom.
  • Music, sound effects and vibration can each be turned off in Settings.
  • The whole game is played with one finger and one kind of tap.
  • Menus and buttons have VoiceOver labels in all seven languages. The gameplay itself needs sight and precise timing, so it cannot be played with VoiceOver alone.
  • Hazards differ by shape and movement as well as color.

Privacy

Naila’s Orbit collects no data, has no ads and works offline. Full policy: Privacy Policy.
